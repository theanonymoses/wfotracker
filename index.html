How to Use

Click once on a date to mark it planned (maroon). Click again to confirm attendance (green). Click a third time to clear it. Use Week-Off Days to grey out recurring days off (e.g. Sat & Sun) across all months. Customise your background in Settings.

Week-Off Days

Select the days of the week that are always off. Every matching date across all months will appear greyed out and won't count towards your goal.
